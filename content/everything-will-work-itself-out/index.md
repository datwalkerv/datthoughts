I often felt like shit, and I would think about why I'm doing this, why I'm doing that, and I started doubting myself.

And recently, at the beginning of the month, I felt kind of at a low point, because August was basically a weaker month. All of my clients were on holiday and stuff like that. And when I was really low like that, a new opportunity came in to work for an American company, which was actually really good, because I knew that this stability would help me a lot right now and would also kickstart my life a bit more, because I could get closer to my goals and buy the Mercedes sooner, which is my biggest priority right now.

And not only did that happen, which I'm really grateful for, but I also managed to close another client for editing, where I'm the one handling the editing, and I'm also getting a relatively large amount of work from them in terms of volume.

And basically, the August desert-version of Bálint, who was kind of depressed and barely doing anything, turned into an all day working from home office person.

I fucking love the whole thing. At first I had to get used to working a bit more strictly and doing more, but now that almost a month has passed, I've gotten so used to it that this amount of work is almost not enough anymore.

I've also implemented AI into my life much more and I'm moving much faster with everything. In the meantime, I still build these dat apps, which I enjoy just as much, and now I'm happy that I'm also more stable with myself.

And even though I'm more tied down than before, I still have that freedom to do whatever, whenever, wherever I want, which is something I would never give up.

I know everything is just a matter of time, and I'm also pushing to get the Mercedes this year. I really hope it works out and that Q4 is going to start really strong.

The lesson is: don't feel like shit and never look at yourself in a negative way. Never look down on yourself. Trust yourself, be grateful, be humble, and everything will work itself out the way it's supposed to.

Keep grinding. Keep putting in the inputs, and the output will just be the reward.

And not gonna lie, I also really loved that this month I went hiking in Romania with a girl. That also recharged me, and it was really good. I really enjoyed it.
