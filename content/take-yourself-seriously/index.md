A lot of times we feel like we don't know what to do or we feel lost, even though I think you always know what you want, even if it's subconsciously. Your brain knows what it wants and probably everything happens the way it is supposed to happen in that exact moment, but still, when I think about it, there have been so many times when I knew what I should do, I was in the right place, at the right time, but for some reason I still didn't do it that way, or I stopped halfway through, or did it differently. But if I had continued and actually done it the way I originally thought I should, I would be much further ahead now, and looking back I can see how well I actually saw the situation and how it wouldn't have been stupid at all to do it that way.

Now this is a personal example, but if I had fully stuck to what I set out to do half a year ago, which was putting my savings into crypto, by now I would have the car I set out to get. Which isn't a problem, because I didn't blow the money on nothing either, because I was careful with it, but instead I went back to my old method, where I put it into an account I can't take the money out of. The end result will be the same, because I'm 100% sure I'll have the car too, but thinking about it, I should have listened to myself. But at the same time, there's also the fact that I didn't take this whole crypto thing that seriously, and there were times when I spent a few hundred euros from it even though I should have put that money into crypto.

I'm not complaining though, because I'm currently 60% up with Solana, which is still a fucking great result, and with a pretty nice amount of money too, it's just that obviously this amount could have been much bigger, and then that +60% would have been bigger too.

The actual lesson from today is that we should take ourselves and our decisions seriously. Because I didn't fully take myself seriously either, only like 80%, and I wasn't always strict about immediately putting x% of my money into it, even though that's exactly what I usually do with this "inaccessible" account. The other thing is that we should trust ourselves, because that can speed up the process.

The lesson for me is that I'm going to put x% of my income aside into crypto, no matter what.
